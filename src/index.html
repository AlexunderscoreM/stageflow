    How to use StageFlow

    • Formations are the blocks on the timeline. Each block starts with a striped movement part (dancers travelling in) followed by the solid hold part.
    • Drag the divider inside a block to set when dancers arrive, and the block's right edge to set when they start moving to the next formation. Edges snap to the playhead, so you can park the playhead on a moment in the music and drag to it. Exact times can be typed in the Formation panel.
    • Switch between 2D, 3D and Split at the top. In 3D, drag to orbit and use the camera buttons to snap back to the audience or judges' view.
    • Put the playhead on a block, then drag dancers on the stage. Shift-click or click-drag a box to select several.
    • With dancers selected, change their colour and shape in the Selection panel on the right.
    • Paths shown are the movement into the current formation. Choose Curved, then drag the round handle to shape the curve.
