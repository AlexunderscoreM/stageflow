Your sets

A set is a show or routine. Each set is made of segments (one formation timeline each), in running order.

Roster

Your dancers with gender, skill and height. Apply a roster to a segment (Dancers tab) or to a whole set — the 3D dancers take each person's height and build, and show their skill.

Dancers

    Shift- or Ctrl-click to select several. Double-click a dancer on stage to rename. Use W A S D to move selected dancers.

    Beats

    The music plays — press Space on every beat. When a new section starts that isn't on an 8-count, press 1 (or Shift + Space) on that beat instead: it's tapped as a beat and the counting restarts at “1” there. Enter or Esc when you're done. Taps replace any beats already in that stretch of the song.

    Section starts (a new “1”)

    Red marks on the music row. The 8-count before a section start can be short (e.g. a 4-count bridge); numbering carries on from it.

    Choreo

    Load a video of someone dancing. StageFlow tracks their skeleton and turns it into a move clip you can put on any dancers in the 3D view.

    Tips: film the whole body head to feet, one dancer, steady camera, good light. The start and end are trimmed automatically if the dancer walks in or out of frame.

    How to use StageFlow

    • The Dashboard lists your sets (shows). Each set holds segments in running order. Click a segment to edit it.
    • Copy & paste: select dancers and press Ctrl+C to copy their spots (nobody selected = the whole formation), go to any formation — in this segment or another — and press Ctrl+V. Ctrl+Shift+V pastes as a new formation, Ctrl+D duplicates the formation. Dancers the segment doesn't have yet are added.
    • Right-click the stage or a formation block for more: paste mirrored, reset to the previous or next formation, mirror, flip, swap two dancers, duplicate, rename, delete.
    • Formations are the blocks on the timeline: a striped movement part, then the solid hold. Drag the divider for arrival and the right edge for leaving. Edges snap to the playhead.
    • Select dancers (click, Shift-click, or drag a box) and move them with the mouse or W A S D. Colour, shape and layout tools are in the Arrange tab on the left.
    • Choreo: analyze a dance video to get a move clip, then put it on dancers — they perform it in the 3D view.
