    How to use StageFlow

    • Formations are the coloured blocks on the timeline. Dancers hold still during a block and travel to the next formation in the gap between blocks.
    • Drag a block to move it, or drag its left/right edges to set when that formation is reached and when the movement to the next one starts.
    • Put the playhead on a block, then drag dancers on the stage to set their positions. Shift-click or drag a box to select several.
    • Paths shown are the movement into the current formation. Select dancers and press Curved, then drag the round handle to shape the curve.
